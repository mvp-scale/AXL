How to kick off a project

MCMaya Chen · Updated 2 days ago · 4 min read

Every project in Northstar starts with a name, an owner and a target date. Teams that agree on these three things in the first week ship noticeably faster than teams that don't -- and they spend less time in status meetings... The kickoff checklist below walks you through each step, including the permissions your collaborators will need and the notifications they'll receive when work is assigned, reviewed or blocked by an unresolved dependency on another team.

⚠️ Note: target dates can be changed later, but every change is recorded in the activity log.

Set a target date

Pick a date your team can defend. A project with a 10 km scope and a 3 week timeline needs a different plan than one with a 12 month runway. Use the target_date field, or press T anywhere in the editor.

"A clear owner is worth more than a perfect plan." — Delivery handbook

Invite your team

  1. Open Settings → Plan & access.
  2. Choose Invite teammate and enter their email.
  3. Pick a role: Viewer, Editor or Owner.
